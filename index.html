About me

My name is Mushtaq Alzuhairi, a licensed real estate agent based in the greater Boston area. Real estate has been an important part of my life for nearly a decade. Over the past nine years, I have been actively involved in buying, selling, owning, and evaluating residential property, building a well-rounded perspective on what creates lasting value and what truly makes a home worth investing in.

My background is in engineering and management. I hold a bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering and a master’s degree in Engineering Management and I have spent more than eight years working across different engineering roles. I currently work as a Project Manager in Surgical Robotics, where I manage complex projects, budgets, timelines, risks, and communication across multiple teams.

That experience shapes how I approach real estate. I pay close attention to market conditions, property conditions, long-term value, and the details that can easily be overlooked during an emotional decision. I enjoy helping people understand those fundamentals so they can make educated informed decisions with confidence, not pressure.

My approach is simple: I represent my clients with the same care and judgment I would use if I were making the decision for myself. I believe a deal should make sense beyond the excitement of the moment. Honesty, transparency, privacy, and clear communication are the standards I hold myself to with every client.
